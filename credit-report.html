What is ugly
  • Depth is nearly nonexistent. Five trade lines ever, three of them open, two of them secured. The largest revolving line this file has ever seen is $408. That reads as starter-tier across the board.
  • The Verizon charge-off is small and unpaid. $1,334 sitting in collection, updated as recently as 10.09.2026, means it is still active and still being reported monthly. This is the single most expensive line on the file relative to its size.
  • The auto loan was settled short. "Settled for less than full balance" plus a 60-day late on a $13,419 note tells the next lender the obligation was abandoned, not completed. That comment sticks for the full reporting window.
  • Both derogatories are recent. 2024 and early 2025. New damage weighs heavier than aged damage, and neither has had time to fade.
  • Thin employment data. Two employers, no hire dates, no income, no titles — not enough to establish stability on paper.
  • No mortgage, no auto, no prime bank card, no installment loan currently open. Zero open non-revolving credit means a thin mix on top of a thin file.
Priority actions — click to mark done
  1. Resolve the Verizon balance first. It is the only live debt and the cheapest possible win. Ask whether it is still with Verizon or placed with an agency, get the payoff in writing, and negotiate a deletion of the collection entry as part of the settlement if they will give it.
  2. Do not dispute anything accurate. The 30s and the settlement are real. Disputing them burns 30 to 45 days and changes nothing.
  3. Grow the secured card, don't stack new ones. Use it, let a small statement balance report, pay it in full. A card reporting $0 every month demonstrates nothing to a scoring model.
  4. Freeze applications for six months. The 09.21.2025 Capital One pull is the most recent. Let the file sit still and age.
  5. Target one unsecured card next, not a second secured one. A single non-secured approval moves this file out of starter territory faster than anything else available.
  6. Get the "JR" suffix consistent. Public records carry it, the bureau file does not. A suffix mismatch on an application is an easy reason for a manual review flag.
  7. Watch the timeline. Both derogatories fall off roughly seven years from their first delinquency, so around 2031 and 2032. Until then, recency is the enemy — every month without new damage improves standing.
Outlook

Salvageable, and not far off. This is a young file with a short, correctable problem list — not a structurally bad one. Clearing $1,334 and letting 18 clean months pass would move it from subprime to respectable on its own. What it cannot survive is another collection or another settled loan.
